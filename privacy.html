Privacy Policy

Sachou Dashboard · Last updated: 27 November 2026

Sachou Dashboard ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by Ali Sachou. It runs only on his own computer and is used only by him to upload and schedule his own videos on his own accounts (Ali Sachou and VAHALAQAH) on YouTube, Instagram, Threads and TikTok. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Information the app accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): the basic details of his own YouTube channels (channel ID, name, handle, profile picture) and permission to upload videos to those channels, including titles, descriptions and thumbnails he writes.
  • Instagram and Threads (Meta APIs): the basic profile details of his own accounts (account ID, username, profile picture), the daily publishing limit, and permission to publish posts he creates.
  • TikTok (TikTok for Developers): the basic profile details of his own accounts (open ID, username, avatar) and permission to send his own videos to those accounts.

The app does not access, collect or store information about viewers, followers, commenters or any other person.

How the information is used

The information is used only to show which account is connected, to check that the connection still works, and to publish the videos, captions and schedules that Ali Sachou creates. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.

Storage and security

Access tokens and settings are stored only on his own computer, in a file readable only by his user account. Videos are sent from his computer directly to each platform. The app has no server, database or analytics of its own.

The app does not use cookies, does not place any files on anyone else's device, and does not show ads or any content served by third parties. This website does not use cookies or trackers either.

Retention and deletion

Stored access tokens are kept until an account is disconnected or the app's settings are deleted, which removes them immediately. Access can also be revoked at any time: for Google (including YouTube) at the Google security settings page, for Instagram and Threads in each app's Settings > Website permissions, and for TikTok in the TikTok app's Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.

When access is revoked, the stored token is removed and any data received from YouTube API Services is deleted within 7 days. The app also checks every hour that each stored token is still valid.

Third-party services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube through the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy. Instagram and Threads data is handled under the Meta Privacy Policy, and TikTok data under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
